In 2026, we paddled the Nation Lakes Canoe Route - upstream and downstream! Saves money on the shuttle, but does make it more strenuous. See our [trip journal](https://viv.batemanzhou.com/2026/08/29/learning-that-canoeing-upstream-is-much-slower-than-canoeing-downstream-yoyoing-the-nations-lakes-route/) on Viv's site.

A quick summary from Viv's blog
> - The longest river section took us 6 hours to upstream. We imagined that all the animals were laughing at us – “look at these idiots going the wrong way!”
> - Surprisingly, the hardest day was not an upstreaming day, but rather one with unforgiving headwind which made us paddle at less than 2km a hour!
> - As far as the local outfitter knows, we’re the first to have yoyo-ed this route
> - The full trip was 10 days, and day 9 was the first day we didn’t get headwind!
> - It was awesome!
